      About Chaaye Khana

      Chaaye Khana is a fine-dining tea house and kitchen on GT Road, near PSO Riverside in Jhelum Cantonment — built around specialty teas, unhurried breakfasts and a full kitchen.

      The place

      The kitchen runs wide — all-day breakfast, Pakistani favourites, Chinese and Continental plates, and BBQ — alongside a tea and beverage list, a bakery counter of cakes and desserts, and steaks.

      The setting

      Guests come to play a round of chess or linger with friends over a pot of tea. Seating runs both indoors and outdoors.

      For families & groups

      Indoor and outdoor seating leaves room for larger tables and family visits.
